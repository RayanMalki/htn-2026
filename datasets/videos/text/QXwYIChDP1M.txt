Today's leaky gut question is, can leaky gut cause Candida overgrowth? Leaky gut is a condition where the integrity of the gut lining is compromised, allowing for the passage of undigested food particles and toxins through the gut wall and into the bloodstream. This can create an environment in which Candida, a type of fungus, can thrive and grow excessively. Candida is a naturally occurring microorganism in the gut, but when it overgrows, it can cause a variety of symptoms such as fatigue, brain fog and digestive issues. There are several factors that can contribute to the development of a leaky gut, including poor diet, stress and certain medications. When the gut is in a state of dysbiosis, meaning an imbalance of beneficial and harmful bacteria, the overgrowth of Candida can happen. This is why treating a leaky gut is important to address Candida overgrowth. Leaky gut can be treated through a combination of dietary changes, probiotics, and supplements.
