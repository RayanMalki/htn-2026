Weight loss myth number 11 Drinking green tea or apple cider vinegar alone leads to weight loss. Some people believe that specific beverages like green tea or apple cider vinegar can lead to significant weight loss results when consumed in isolation. The fact of the matter is these drinks may have minor effects. While green tea and apple cider vinegar may have some minor metabolic benefits, they won't lead to substantial weight loss on their own.
