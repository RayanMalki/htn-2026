Prebiotics, for example, fiber and vegetables, help feed the good bacteria in your body. Probiotics are the good bacterial colonies that reside in your microbiome that help produce serotonin, metabolites, and good things that your body consumes. Postbiotics are really interesting because they are the metabolites, the byproducts, of probiotics. And these are really important because they're the main fuel cell for the digestive cells in your tract. And they also have profound impacts on inflammation and brain health. We chose to create a delivery system that combines two capsules to help ensure the delivery of our prebiotic and our postbiotic in different parts of the digestive cell.
