Hello and welcome to our video on the benefits of ashwagandha for cortisol. Cortisol is a hormone that is produced by the adrenal gland in response to stress. It is commonly referred to as the stress hormone. Elevated levels of cortisol can lead to a number of health problems, including weight gain, fatigue, and decreased immune function. Research suggests that ashwagandha can help to reduce cortisol levels in the body. This is because ashwagandha is believed to have a calming effect on the nervous system, which can help to reduce stress and anxiety. When it comes to dosage, it is recommended to take ashwagandha supplements at a dose of 300 to 500 milligrams per day. However, it is important to check with your doctor before starting, especially if you are taking any medications or have any underlying medical conditions.
