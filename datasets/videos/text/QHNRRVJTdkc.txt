It's called interval walking, or Japanese walking, and the science behind it is blowing up. Instead of a long, boring stroll, you alternate three minutes of fast, brisk walking with three minutes of slow, relaxed walking. Repeat this for just 30 minutes. Why? It spikes your heart rate, forces your body to adapt, and improves your VO2 max significantly faster than steady state cardio. Plus, studies show it's incredible. So next time you go for a walk, don't just plod along. Push the pace, then recover. Your heart and your waistline will thank you.
