So a question I've had a lot lately, does yo-yo dieting permanently damage your metabolism? A commonly heard claim is that the process of losing weight or fat and then regaining it, often called yo-yo dieting, can permanently wreck your metabolism or make fat loss harder in the long run. Now one way this might happen would be due to your body composition worsening after a cycle of weight loss and regain. So if someone lost a significant amount of muscle due to poor dieting practices and failed to regain it after the diet, they would end up at a higher body fat percentage, even at the same weight, which could impact their energy expenditure. But this would actually only be a very small difference. Another that might cause this, especially for women, is if the dilution of body fat changed. If fat were lost from a relatively easy to lose area, but regained in a more difficult to lose area, this might have a long term negative effect. This could happen to a relatively small degree. It's far more likely that those people with slow metabolism are in a much harder starting position for fat loss, although it might get worse with repeated cycles of dieting. Alternatively, one of the hormones that regulates metabolic rate might never cover fully. That said, direct studies show that repeated cycles of weight loss and regain seem to have no long lasting impact on most components of energy expenditure. So the changes when weight and fat are lost are reversed when the weight and fat are regained.
