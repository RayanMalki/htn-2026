We all love junk food, fries, burgers, pizza. But here's the truth. It's not just empty calories. Eating junk food regularly can mess with your brain, making you crave more. It can spike your blood sugar, cause inflammation, and even affect your mood. Your gut health suffers, your energy drops, and your body starts storing fat more easily. Start by swapping one meal a day, drinking water, and adding protein and fiber. Remember, your body doesn't hate you. It's just asking for real fuel. Choose wisely.
