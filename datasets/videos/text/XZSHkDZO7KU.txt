Raw or unpasteurized milk can contain dangerous germs such as salmonella, E. coli, listeria, and others that can cause serious health problems such as vomiting, diarrhea, dehydration, and flu-like symptoms. The most severe outcomes can potentially lead to paralysis, kidney failure, stroke, or even death. That's why milk is pasteurized, which means that it's heated to 161 degrees for just 15 seconds, which kills the bacteria living in it. Pasteurization process is safe and does not damage the nutrients in the milk. So why would someone want to drink raw milk even though the CDC and FDA warn against it? Well, small studies paid for by the Raw Milk Institute claim that raw milk can help prevent asthma and treat digestive issues like IBS and Crohn's disease. But the research is poorly done, and the risk of bacteria outbreak far outweighs the supposed benefits. This is especially true for younger and older people. And those with weakened immune systems who cannot fight off infection as easily. Drinking milk has many health benefits such as strengthening your bones and muscles and providing many nutrients and vitamins for your body. Stick to drinking pasteurized milk and skip the risky side effects that come with raw milk.
