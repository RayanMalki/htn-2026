You're not grounding for 30 minutes a day. You could be missing out on some pretty awesome benefits. Grounding or earthing improves sleep, normalizes your circadian rhythm, shifts your autonomic nervous system from sympathetic to parasympathetic activation, or in other words, it takes you out of fight or flight mode. It reduces inflammation and pain, it increases heart rate variability, and it even reduces your blood viscosity. All you need to do is walk around barefoot or lie on the ground for 30 minutes a day. I do it in my yard, but you can even do it on sand, grass, unsealed pavement, or even in the body of water. You can also get equipment like mats and sobs that you just plug in. If walking around barefoot outside gives you the ache, there are grounding shoes. Most of them are pretty expensive because they incorporate materials like copper and silver, but here's a little hack. You can get some leather-souled ballet flats or even some wool slippers. Once they absorb a little bit of moisture from the ground or from perspiration, they're going to become really conductive, and that's going to be a grounding shoe for less. I do want to add the caveat that the research is not super solid, a lot of it is not super solid. It was done without a control group, but anecdotally, a lot of people have experienced tangible benefits and it doesn't hurt to try.
