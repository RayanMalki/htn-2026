If you are into Whole Foods, you may have heard of Western A Price, a dental surgeon whose book Nutrition and Physical Degeneration was written back in 1939. Before this, he visited many tribes and remote villages around the world. He was searching for the dietary differences between Westerners and tribal health. The village people ate traditional Whole Foods, with many of these coming from the livestock they cultivated or found in the wild. In Switzerland, the people had sturdy physiques, strong immunity to dental cavities, strong immunity against degenerative diseases, they ate summer-made raw cheese made with raw goat or raw cow's milk.
