Arthweight management is a vegetarian tablet formulated to aid fat transport and utilization and to support energy and endurance. Especially useful alongside a calorie-aware diet and regular exercise. The formula centers on elkarnatine tartrate with 1,500 milligrams provided per two tablets serving. Arth positions the product as a non-stimulant option, i.e. no caffeine jitters, designed to pair with active lifestyles. elkarnatine is a naturally occurring compound that helps transport long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria. The energy centers of cells, where they can be oxidized, burned, for fuel. This is why elkarnatine is often called a fat transporter. By improving the body's ability to mobilize and utilize stored fat, especially during exercise, elkarnatine can support endurance, energy production, and body composition goals.
