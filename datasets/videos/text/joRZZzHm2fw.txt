If we look deeper into the issue and we look at what actually causes decay, it's the sugar. It's always the sugar. It's the frequency of sugar intake, which is really our modern diets. So if you have a healthy diet, which is fruits, vegetables, whole grains and legumes, your teeth will not be susceptible to decay. So there is no need fluoride in that situation. So my recommendation is not necessarily to go for a fluoridated toothpaste or drink fluoridated water. Have a healthy diet.
