Working out at 6 AM might actually be making you hold onto belly fat. Here's why. It comes down to bio-syncing, matching your workouts to your body's natural cortisol and hormone rhythms. If you're highly stressed and waking up exhausted, doing a high intensity hit workout at 6 AM spikes your cortisol even further, telling your body to store fat. Instead, try low impact mobility or yoga in the morning to gently wake your nervous system. Save the heavy lifting and high intensity cardio for the late afternoon when your core body temperature peaks and your joints are fully lubricated. Work with your biology, not against it.
