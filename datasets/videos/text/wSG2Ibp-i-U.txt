Hey everyone, welcome back. Let's talk about another hot topic in ophthalmology. Are these blue light blocking glasses protected for your eyes? Let's find out. You've probably seen ads all over the internet that claim that these blue light blocking glasses can be protected for your eyes. For that reason, the American Academy of Ophthalmology does not recommend blue light blocking glasses. There are two big studies cited that help back up this recommendation. The first looked at whether our iPads, iPhones, or computer screens emitted harmful UV radiation and they found that no. There was no substantial amount of UVA or UVB radiation coming from our devices. The next study assessed whether these blue light blocking glasses can help minimize digital eye strain. And the answer is also, no, it doesn't. That being said, there are ways to minimize digital eye strain. What I always recommend is blink. Dry eyes is commonly linked to digital eye strain. Two, think about the 2020-2020 rule. Every 20 minutes, take 20 seconds to look at something 20 feet away. Lastly, think about ergonomics. Adjust the brightness of your screen. Try to using anti-glare screen protectors and adjust your computer height so it sits just right below your eye level. If you want to spend 10, 20 bucks to wear these glasses when you work, go ahead. Just don't expect to see a clinical benefit. Anyways, I hope you found that helpful. We'll see you next time.
