Most probiotic bacteria don't survive passage through your stomach acid. And even the ones that do rarely colonize permanently, they're gone within days of stopping. What actually heals your gut is what those bacteria produce. Short-chain fatty acids, especially butyrate. Butyrate is the primary fuel for the cells lining your colon. Without it, your gut lining becomes permeable, leaky gut, and systemic inflammation follows. You don't get water rate from a pill. You get it by feeding your existing bacteria the fiber they need. Cooked and cooled potatoes and rice, green bananas, oats, garlic, and leeks are the highest producers. Probiotics can complement this, but fiber comes first. Always.
