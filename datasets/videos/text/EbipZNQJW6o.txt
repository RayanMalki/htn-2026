Managing weight is one of the most common health goals for people worldwide. With rising cases of obesity, metabolic disorders, and lifestyle related illnesses, many are turning towards natural methods of maintaining a healthy weight. The gut, often referred to as the second brain, is central to your overall health and well-being. It is home to trillions of microorganisms, collectively called the gut microbiota. In digestion, regulate metabolism, produce essential vitamins, and influence hormones linked to hunger and satiety. Dietary fiber plays a vital role in gut health and weight management. It adds bulk to food, keeps you full for longer, and slows down sugar absorption, preventing insulin spikes. Moreover, fiber acts as a prebiotic, feeding the good bacteria in your gut.
