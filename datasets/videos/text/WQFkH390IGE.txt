A MTHFR gene uses a sample of your blood to look for changes in the MTHFR gene. You have two copies of the MTHFR gene, one from each parent. The gene is providing instructions for making the MTHFR protein. Now this protein helps your body use folate. It needs folate to break down homocysteine. Now, normally your body quickly breaks down homocysteine, so you have very little in your blood stream. But if you have a change in MTHFR gene, the MTHFR proteins may not process folate properly. As a result, you may have increased levels of homocysteine in your blood. This test is used to look for the two most common MTHFR changes cold, C6, double 7T and A1298C. Why and when this test is needed? It should be ordered if a blood test showed higher than normal levels of homocysteine. What was diagnosed with an MTHFR gene change? You and close family members have early heart or blood vessel diseases. This was all thanks for watching.
