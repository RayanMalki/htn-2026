Struggling with a constant phlegmy throat you just can't clear? It's incredibly annoying. Let's explore what's really causing all that mucus. Constant throat mucus is often LPR. That's a type of silent reflux where stomach acid travels high enough to irritate your throat. That low stomach acid stops the stomach valve from closing properly, letting acid leak up to your vocal cords. Standard medicine states that reflux is typically caused by a weak stomach valve, obesity, or trigger foods, not low acid. Dr. Bix suggests taking betaine hydrochloride to increase acid while cutting out grains and intermittent fasting to heal your gut. Be careful. Self-treating with high-dose acid can severely burn your stomach lining if you have an ulcer. Always talk to a doctor first. Don't guess with your health. To beat chronic phlegm safely, avoid trigger foods and work with a doctor to find the real cause.
