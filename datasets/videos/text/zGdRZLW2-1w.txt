When you think about histamine, what comes to mind is probably allergies. Our bodies make histamine, and it's also found in some foods like canned fish, avocados, and spinach. When our bodies produce too much, it's called histamine intolerance and could damage the lining of your gut. Gas, bloating, nausea, diarrhea, even constipation, abdominal pain. These are symptoms that are pretty vague and can look a lot like something like irritable bowel syndrome or IBS. You might have histamine intolerance. Remove some foods from your diet to see if the symptoms resolve. Taking histamine out of the diet is the primary way for you and I as individuals to determine whether histamine intolerance is playing a role in the gastrointestinal symptoms that we experience.
