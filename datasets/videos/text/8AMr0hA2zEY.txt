If you've ever had anything to do with intermittent fasting, or nutrition in general, you may have come across the word autophagy. But what is it exactly? Autophagy. A mysterious word with a pretty interesting meaning. The word autophagy, translated from ancient Greek autophagus, means to devour oneself. And in fact, that's exactly what happens during autophagy. Our cells start to process themselves. The result? More efficient cells that can improve our life quality and extend our life expectancy. Those who'd like to benefit from this are recommended to use the 16-8 intermittent fasting method. For example, the autophagy process starts around 16 hours of fasting. Autophagy is not an advertising ploy. And this became clear by 2016. That year, cell biologist Yoshinori Osumi won their Nobel Prize for his groundbreaking research into this vital process. Thanks to him, the existence of autophagy was finally recognized worldwide. Which means intermittent fasting even carries a Nobel Prize around its neck, somewhat indirectly. For the coffee lovers out there, coffee contains a substance that stimulates autophagy, meaning coffee can actually be beneficial during the fasting phase. However, animal proteins inhibit autophagy, which is why coffee should be drunk black. That, and it breaks your fast.
