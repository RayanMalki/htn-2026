Doing hundreds of crunches is destroying your spine. Do this one move instead. Meet the plank hover. Crunches put dangerous repetitive strain on your spinal discs. The plank hover, where you hold a strict braced plank just inches off the floor, engages your entire deep core, the transverse abdominis. This is the muscle that actually pulls your waist in tight and protects your lower back. Drop to your forearms, squeeze your glutes, brace your stomach like you're about to be punched and hold it. Just 30 seconds of this is infinitely better for your core strength and posture than 50 sloppy sit-ups.
