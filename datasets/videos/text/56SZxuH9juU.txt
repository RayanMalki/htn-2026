The adrenal glands are above the kidneys and are responsible for creating hormones that regulate various bodily functions. Problems can arise when there is a mass or nodule in the adrenal gland that creates too many hormones. Signs and symptoms of adrenal problems include high blood pressure, Adrenal problems can cause high blood pressure that is difficult to control. This is the most common sign of an adrenal problem, especially when the blood pressure is difficult to control despite being on many medications for it. Weight gain. Adrenal problems can lead to weight gain, especially in the abdominal area and face. Muscle weakness. Adrenal problems can cause muscle weakness, especially in the legs. Mood changes. Adrenal problems can cause mood swings, irritability, and anxiety. Skin changes. Adrenal problems can cause changes in the skin, such as thinning, easy bruising, and stretch marks. Irregular periods. Adrenal problems can lead to irregular menstrual periods in women. And finally, adrenal problems can cause increased thirst and urination. If you are having these symptoms and are concerned about your adrenal health, you should speak to your regular doctor. These symptoms are not very specific, which means that there are many things that could cause them. Your doctor will help you decide if you should have testing for an adrenal issue, which usually means drawing blood work to check your hormone levels.
