Ashwagandha, often referred to as Indian Jinseng in traditional Ayurvedic literature, is a powerful herb that has been used for centuries to strengthen the body and mind. The botanical name with ania somnifera may sound unfamiliar, but today its benefits are widely recognized across the world. While Himalaya Ashwagandha is not marketed primarily as a weight loss supplement, it can support weight loss efforts indirectly through several mechanisms. When you're stressed, your body releases more cortisol, often called the stress hormone. High cortisol is linked to cravings for sugary foods, higher appetite, and fat storage around the belly. Ashwagandha's adaptogenic properties help normalize cortisol levels, reducing stress-related eating and the hormonal drive toward weight gain. While not a direct weight loss pill, Ashwagandha can be a valuable ally in your weight management journey.
