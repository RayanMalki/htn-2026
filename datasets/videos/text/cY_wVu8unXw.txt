Ashwagandha has been used for centuries for its potential to boost mental acuity and overall health. But only now are scientific studies able to confirm its benefits for brain power, memory and cognition. Ashwagandha works by modulating the body's stress response through its active compounds called with vanolides, which have robust anti-inflammatory, anti-stress and neuroprotective properties. These compounds regulate cortisol, the primary stress hormone, thereby mitigating the cognitive impairments associated with chronic stress. Ashwagandha also promotes neuron health by enhancing dendrite growth, crucial for nerve cell communication, thus boosting cognition and memory. A 2017 double blind study showed that participants administered with 300 milligrams of Ashwagandha root extract twice daily exhibited significant improvements in memory. A 2014 study further backed these cognitive enhancing properties, revealing improved cognitive and psychomotor performance in those given Ashwagandha. While the recommended dosage ranges from 300 to 500 milligrams of high concentration full spectrum Ashwagandha root extract daily, it is crucial to consult with a healthcare provider for individual guidance. Given its potential to improve cognitive function, regulate stress response and promote brain health, Ashwagandha presents as a promising natural solution for those seeking to enhance their mental capabilities.
