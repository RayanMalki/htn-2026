One concern that some people have is about the bioavailability of plant-based proteins in humans. Basically, there have been concerns that plant-based proteins are not digested and absorbed as well by humans as animal proteins are. This means that even if you're eating the same amount of protein, it may not be used by the body as well. This idea probably came from some past research that indicated that plant proteins might have a much lower digestibility in humans than the animal proteins. However, the newer and more accurate research has shown almost no difference between the digestibility of animal proteins versus plant proteins, with the difference being only a small percentage. And this was still seen even with the plant-based proteins being in less than optimal conditions in the study, which presents the next common myth that plant-based proteins are not digested as well by humans as animal proteins.
