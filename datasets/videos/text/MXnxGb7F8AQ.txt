When your ovaries stop producing hormones, the process of primary menopause and menopause, what actually happens is that the only place your hormones are coming from are your adrenals. And I always knew that you had to manage your stress in order to have a successful menopause and to not die of pain and the trauma and the horrendousness of primary menopause. So all the stress responses that you have in place that didn't matter because it was your ovaries creating hormones, now it's your adrenals making your hormones and only your adrenals. So all the stress you've got is hitting those adrenals and stopping them functioning well, and so you start to feel terrible. So if you don't manage your stress hormones, you can't actually have your stress. You have to succeed at managing your perimenopause symptoms. So no matter what else you do, you've got to manage your stress hormones.
