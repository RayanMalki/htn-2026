If that gap widens, more than it should, there are two implications. Firstly, it means that any toxins from the gut that were meant to get excreted can go into the bloodstream and then through the bloodstream can affect anywhere in the body. Secondly, it also means that nutrients from the blood can be excreted through the gut, meaning we could be taking in the right nutrients, the right food, but if there is this leaky gut, those nutrients will get lost.
