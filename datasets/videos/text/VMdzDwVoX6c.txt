Many parents wonder whether or not they should give their children fluoride supplements. And this is a good question. It's going to depend on whether or not the water you drink in your area is fluorinated or not. And it's also going to depend on how old your child is and whether or not their breastfed or formula fed. Children who are less than six months of age don't need any type of fluoride supplementation. But between six months and two years children are getting teeth and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that children have a smooth ear of toothpaste just a very very very small amount on a soft brush and brush their teeth twice a day. Any teeth that are in their mouth and they will get a small amount of fluoride from this. Now above and beyond the toothpaste again it depends on whether or not the water in your area is fluorinated. If your baby is bottle fed and getting tap water and it's fluorinated a supplement may not be necessary. But if your baby is breastfed then it's a possibility. So again check with your doctor and they'll be able to give you the best recommendation depending on your circumstance.
