So leaky gut syndrome is not an actual clinically thought of entity. There is no study that has shown that there is such thing as a leaky gut. That being said, there is evolving research that's showing that maybe we have more intolerance or inflammation to certain allergens or certain things that we take in, such as lactose or gluten. These may not be true deficiencies or allergies, such as celiac disease, but we may be intolerant to certain foods. The idea then is that the gut is more sensitive and may be leaking certain things out into the system. Now that's never been proven, but it's the concept and there's a lot of research in this area.
