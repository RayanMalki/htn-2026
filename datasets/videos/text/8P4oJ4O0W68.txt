How to Lose Belly Fat Fast Whenever we talk about losing fat, diet and exercise are generally the main topics discussed. But when you're not seeing results, there are other factors you need to consider, especially when it comes to losing belly fat. Excessive fat in the belly can be caused by high cortisol levels. Cortisol is a steroid hormone naturally released from your adrenal glands and serves as the body's primary stress hormone that aids to regulate essential functions. High cortisol levels can contribute to weight gain that's predominantly concentrated in the abdomen area. To get adequate sleep Getting enough sleep allows the body to rest and rejuvenate from the day to day. Sleep deprivation is one of the biggest stressors on your body. Not getting adequate sleep typically noted as 7 to 10 hours. can significantly increase your cortisol levels. Not only can that lead to weight gain, but can also lead to more sleep deficiency creating one vicious cycle. Having healthy daily sleep habits that improve your sleep is the best way to get your cortisol levels in balance. Make sure you add these two tips to your diet and exercise plan.
