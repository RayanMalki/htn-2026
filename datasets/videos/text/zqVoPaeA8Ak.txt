You've heard it before. Good nutrition can reduce stress. But do you believe it? Let's explore two clinically-studied nutrients that have been shown to help your body respond to stress. First up, magnesium. About 50% of Americans do not get enough magnesium, which can cause your brain to get stuck in a stressed state. This mighty mineral helps your brain return back to normal after a stressful situation. In a clinical trial, daily magnesium supplementation significantly decreased anxiety and depression symptoms. Next up, ashwaganduru. When stressed, your body releases a hormone called cortisol, which puts you into fight or flight mode. Unfortunately, the frequent stressors of modern life leave many people stuck in a state of chronic stress, constantly flooded with destructive levels of cortisol. In a clinical trial, daily supplementation of ashwaganduru extract significantly lowered cortisol levels, thereby increasing quality of life scores. When life feels overwhelming, supplementation of magnesium and ashwaganduru extract can help you better respond to the stress.
