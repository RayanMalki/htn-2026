Trying to lose weight, but nothing's working. The problem might not be food or exercise. It could be sleep and stress. Poor sleep changes your hunger hormones, making you crave junk food and slowing fat loss. High stress raises cortisol, which makes your body store fat, especially around your belly. Even with a perfect diet, bad sleep and constant stress can keep the weight from coming off. Prioritize better sleep and stress management to finally see results.
