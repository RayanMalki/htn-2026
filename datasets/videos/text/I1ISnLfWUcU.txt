You may have heard about autophagy when it comes to intermittent fasting. The question is, do you need to get into autophagy and what is it? Autophagy means self-eating is a natural process and involves breaking down unneeded or damaged components within a cell and reusing them as building blocks for cellular repair in our gut and in our body. Autophagy in essence helps keep the cells of our bodies healthy. I talked about cream in the coffee. Does coffee with cream stop autophagy? Yes. This is because any coffee that contains calories from cream, sugar or butter will stop the autophagy process unless it's less than 10 calories and shifts the body from waste removal to the digestive process.
