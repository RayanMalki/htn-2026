There are many factors that cause stress in our body and mind, including poor diet, too much sugar, alcohol, caffeine, nicotine, over-training and work and social pressures. When we're stressed, our nervous system and many of the chemical messengers in our brains are impacted, leaving us feeling drained, lacking in energy, anxious and even contributing to gut problems like changes in gut bacteria levels, bloating, gas and irritable bowel syndrome. This is key to managing symptoms of stress and the ECS is a major part of this connection. This means supporting gut health through diet and supplementing with gut bacteria as well as supporting ECS function as part of a lifestyle plan may help to manage feelings and symptoms of stress and anxiety.
