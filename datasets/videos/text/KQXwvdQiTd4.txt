What we know actually from our adrenals, which are basically the two glands that sit on top of our kidneys and produce cortisol, is that they actually have a receptor for interleukin-6, which is a type of in pro-inflammatory cytokine. So if things are causing inflammation and causing cytokine release in the body, such as infection, toxins, food sensitivities, are going to cause interleukin-6 to be released and can then bind to these receptors on the adrenal glands.
