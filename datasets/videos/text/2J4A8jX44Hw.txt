Milk has been a staple in our diet, but is milk even good for you? Milk has multiple benefits such as providing us the essential amino acids, vitamins and minerals. However, there are some cons to consuming milk. Studies have shown some correlations to prostate cancer. High intake of calcium and increased IGF, which is a hormone that promotes bone and tissue development, correlate with prostate cancer. They also correlate with early menarchy, the area which women reach sexual maturity. Other than cow's milk, there are various alternatives that are fortified with nutrients. However, each of the alternatives have their own cons to them.
